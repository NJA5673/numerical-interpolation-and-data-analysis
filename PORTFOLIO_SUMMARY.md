# Portfolio entry

**Numerical Interpolation and Data Analysis** · MATLAB · Numerical methods

I developed a collection of MATLAB functions and experiments to study interpolation, polynomial evaluation, numerical differentiation, and least squares fitting. I compared Lagrange and Neville interpolation, validated Horner's method against MATLAB polynomial functions, and investigated how step size affects finite-difference derivatives. A simulated projectile example shows how small errors in position measurements can become much more visible after computing velocity and acceleration.

The project strengthened my ability to connect mathematical formulas to working code, test implementations against known answers, and explain when a numerical estimate can be misleading. The data in the projectile example are synthetic and reproducible.

**Featured results:** The acceleration plot illustrates how second differences magnify small position errors. A series of interpolation plots compares polynomial degrees, with a degree-20 overlay showing a close match for the chosen function and interval. Both examples highlight why a numerical result needs to be interpreted in context.
